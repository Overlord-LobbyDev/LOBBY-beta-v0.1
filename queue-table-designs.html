Queue · table designs

Five directions, drawn at the size the room actually gives them, in the app's own colours. The accent is hard-coded to Counter-Strike's orange here — in the app it is extracted from whatever game the table is for, so every one of these would take the game's colour automatically.

01Match-lobby cards

No table at all. The pre-game language of Valorant, League and Rocket League: players are portrait cards, the centre column carries the count and the code. Speaking lights a card from its base like a stage light. Scales from 2 to 10 without ever looking like a smudge, and the empty seat is unmistakably an empty seat.

05Hybrid · cards in a HUD frame

01 and 02 disagree about one thing only — whether a player is a card or a node — and agree about everything else. This keeps the cards, because they hold a name, a role, a rank and a ping without crowding, and puts them inside 02's bracketed frame with the count and code on the rule above. Corners are cut off the cards so they belong to the frame rather than sitting on it.
